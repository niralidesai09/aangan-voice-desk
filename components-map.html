The flywheel: who closes the loop
Call answered→Qualified + booked→Designer taps Accept · Called · Consult done→ Won (₹ value) / Lost (reason)→Dashboard→Weekly tune of prompt + rubric→ Won client asked for referral→More calls

Telegram over email: a push alert matters when answering in under an hour converts 4×, and the buttons turn every outcome into data in one tap. That's what lets the dashboard show revenue, not just calls.
